# Product

## What it is
The Idea Genealogist: type any concept (Stoicism, Democracy, Entropy) and Gemini returns its intellectual family tree. Roots (ancestors), a Trunk (the person/work that codified it), Branches (modern descendants) and a Rival (the opposing idea). Portraits and artwork come from Wikipedia. Built in 2025 as a portfolio project.

## Audience and use
Primary: recruiters and hiring engineers viewing this inside a portfolio. They give it ten seconds, type one concept, and judge craft. It must be self-explanatory on arrival and the results page must photograph well. Secondary: curious people clicking through ideas.

## Mode
Experience. The generated lineage is the artifact and the interface recedes around it.

## Brand commitments (set by owner, 2026-10)
- Direction: archive / museum. Gallery wall-text and object-label grammar, not a tech product.
- Must not look AI-generated or "vibe coded": no neon, glow, glass, gradient text, mono-as-costume, particle backgrounds, tilt cards.
- Keep the flow of knowledge: concept -> roots -> trunk -> branches -> rival. Layout may change.
- Keep: light/dark theme, export-to-image, section navigator, click-through to trace any node.
- Real images are non-negotiable: every entry shows a picture or a deliberate typographic placeholder, never silent holes.

## Constraints
Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind 4, framer-motion, next-themes. Gemini 2.5 Flash for content. No paid image APIs (Google Custom Search key expired).
