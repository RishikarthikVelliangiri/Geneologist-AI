# Surface: home + lineage results (Experience)

Audience: recruiters, 10 seconds. Job: type a concept, see a believable, beautiful lineage.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The lineage reads as a museum catalogue of one idea: wall text and object labels on a quiet gallery wall, with a single drawn spine that carries the reader from ancestors to descendants. Refuses: neon cyber cards, glass, glow, gradient text, tilt, particles.
OWN-WORLD: Cool pale stone wall (dark: ink-blue night room), near-black ink, one ultramarine accent used as a flat slab behind the codifier and for the spine. Libre Caslon Display for names and titles, Hanken Grotesk for label and body. Hairline rules, square-cornered plates with captions, no shadows, no pills.
STORY: Visitor grasps "every idea has a family tree" from a schematic diagram, types a concept, scrolls down a drawn spine from roots through the ultramarine codifier to branches and finally the rival, and can trace any name onward.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, large Caslon headline "Every idea has a family tree." with one-line explainer and a wide underlined search field with a Trace button and example concepts. Right, an authored SVG schematic of roots/trunk/branches/rival. Slim masthead with wordmark and theme toggle. Results: concept title, lede and a captioned plate side by side.
FORM: Museum catalogue / wall label, chosen by the owner (pinned, no roll). Code-led. Seed key: pinned-by-user.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
